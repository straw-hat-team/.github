# Support

This document explains where to get help.

Read our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before participating. By interacting with our
repositories and organizations, you agree to abide by its terms.

## Where to ask

- Questions, ideas, and open-ended conversations belong in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/straw-hat-team/discussions).
- Bugs and feature requests belong in the issue tracker of the repository in question.
- Security vulnerabilities follow our [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md). Never report them publicly.

## Asking good questions

Help us help you. The time you spend framing a question is almost always less than the time everyone
spends untangling a vague one.

- Search first. Someone may have asked already.
- [Talk to a duck](https://rubberduckdebugging.com) before you hit send.
- Watch out for the [XY problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XY_problem). Tell us what you are
  actually trying to achieve, not only the approach you got stuck on.
- Say what you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
- Include a minimal reproduction. A link to a repository or a runnable snippet beats a description.
- Include versions: the package, the language runtime, and the operating system.
- Paste errors and logs as text. Screenshots are welcome, but not as a replacement for text.
